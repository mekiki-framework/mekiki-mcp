## Attribution for reuse of repository files

When reusing or adapting the repository files themselves, provide attribution substantially in the following form:

> Kengo Tomita, *Mekiki Framework T1–T5 AI-Readable Corpus*, https://mekiki-framework.github.io/, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Canonical scholarly sources: the T1 through T5 DOIs listed above.

Indicate whether changes were made. Attribution to the repository does not replace citation of the relevant paper DOI for scholarly claims.

## Local citation namespaces

Author–date suffixes belong to each paper's own References, not to the corpus order. For example, T3 uses Tomita (2026a) for T1 and Tomita (2026b) for T2, whereas T5 uses Tomita (2026a) for T3 and Tomita (2026b) for T4. Do not infer a paper number from a suffix. Preserve the cited work's title and DOI.

The DOI record is the scholarly citation target. The repository's Markdown and HTML copies are derivative reading views. A source hash verifies equality with the supplied file, not DOI availability, peer review, or substantive correctness. Publication and peer-review assertions remain those provided by the author; this build performs no DOI-resolution audit.
